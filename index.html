About EF Nexus

Africa's Backbone of Educational Transformation

EduFacilitators Nexus (EF Nexus) is a dynamic powerhouse company that serves as the institutional backbone of transformative education, AI-driven consulting, and innovation across Africa and beyond.

  • Parent company and strategic backbone of NIIT (Nexus Institute of Innovation and Technology) — a premier learning institution
  • Operates the Pathora University Preparation Program — international university access consultancy for African students
  • Delivering hybrid learning, AI systems, and digital education support to thousands across Sub-Saharan Africa Countries
  • Strategic partnerships with global institutions, NGOs, and government agencies for sustainable development
  • Committed to youth empowerment, digital skills, and bridging the education gap in underserved communities
