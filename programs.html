Pathora University Preparation Program

The Pathora University Preparation Program is EF Nexus's flagship international education consultancy service — helping African students access world-class universities across the UK, USA, Canada, Europe, Australia, and beyond.

From initial assessment and university selection to application writing, scholarship guidance, visa support, and pre-departure orientation — we walk with every student on their international education journey.

Pathora Process

How Pathora Works

A 6-step journey from aspiration to international enrollment.

1

Free Initial Assessment

We evaluate your academic background, aspirations, language proficiency, and financial situation to determine the best pathway for you.

2

University Shortlisting

Based on your profile, we curate a tailored list of universities and programs that match your academic goals, budget, and career aspirations.

3

Application Support

Expert guidance on personal statements, reference letters, academic transcripts, and all application documents for multiple universities.

4

Scholarship Guidance

Identification and application support for relevant scholarships, bursaries, and funding opportunities to make international education affordable.

5

Visa Assistance

Complete student visa application guidance including documentation, interview preparation, and compliance for your destination country.

6

Pre-Departure & Settling

Orientation on life abroad, accommodation guidance, banking setup, and connections to EF Nexus's international student community.
