Flagship Division

NIIT — Nexus Institute of Innovation & Technology

The Nexus Institute of Innovation and Technology (NIIT) is EF Nexus's premier educational institution — a transformative learning hub delivering industry-relevant, technology-forward education to the next generation of African professionals, innovators, and entrepreneurs.

NIIT combines rigorous academic programs with practical, hands-on learning experiences, mentorship, and career pathways designed to make graduates globally competitive while remaining locally impactful.
