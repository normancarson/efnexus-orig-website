Who We Are

EF Nexus — A Powerhouse of Education & Innovation

EduFacilitators Nexus Ltd (EF Nexus) is Africa's premier education holding company — a dynamic, multi-division enterprise that serves as the institutional backbone of transformative learning, AI innovation, and strategic consulting.

Founded with a bold vision to bridge the education gap in Sub-Saharan Africa and connect the continent to global opportunities, EF Nexus operates through specialized divisions that collectively empower students, institutions, businesses, and communities.

We are the parent company and strategic backbone of NIIT (Nexus Institute of Innovation and Technology), and we proudly operate the Pathora University Preparation Program for African students seeking international academic access.

Our Direction

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be the most transformative education and innovation ecosystem in Africa — a continent where every individual, regardless of background, has access to world-class learning, technology, and opportunities for a prosperous future.

Our Mission

To facilitate transformative education, drive AI-powered innovation, and deliver strategic consulting solutions that empower African students, institutions, and businesses to compete globally and lead locally — creating a deep and broad facilitation network that bridges gaps and unlocks potential.

Our Journey

The Story of EF Nexus

2024

The Foundation

EduFacilitators Nexus Ltd was established with a bold vision: to become the backbone of education transformation in Africa. Starting with a small but passionate team, EF Nexus began laying the groundwork for what would become a continent-wide education network.

2024

NIIT is Born

EF Nexus establishes the Nexus Institute of Innovation and Technology (NIIT) as its flagship educational institution, launching its first cohort of technology and business programs to serve African students seeking world-class education.

2025

Pathora Program Launch

The Pathora University Preparation Program is launched, providing African students with comprehensive consultancy support to access international universities in the UK, USA, Canada, Europe, and beyond.

2026

Continental Expansion

EF Nexus expands its footprint across Sub-Saharan Africa, establishing partnerships with over 30 institutions and reaching students in 10+ countries. The AI Innovation Lab is officially launched.

2026

The Future is Now

With 500+ students empowered, 15+ institutional partners, and AI-driven programs in 6+ countries, EF Nexus stands stronger than ever — committed to shaping Africa's educational and economic future for generations to come.
